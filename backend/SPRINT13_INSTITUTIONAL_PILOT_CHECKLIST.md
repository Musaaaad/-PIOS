# Sprint 13 Institutional Pilot Checklist

1. Approve pilot charter, committee charter, conflict policy, and named authority.
2. Bind real OIDC identities; store only subject hashes, roles, and site scope.
3. Attest institutional data sources, checksums, period, record count, and deidentification.
4. Confirm no open operational P0/P1 incident.
5. Activate the pilot only after all gates pass.
6. Link governed review sessions and preserve minutes and COI evidence.
7. Record human decisions and independent quality reviews.
8. Measure operational outcomes, adoption, and privacy-safe feedback.
9. Complete the pilot only when sessions, quality reviews, and metrics are complete.
10. Generate, approve, hash, and publish the outcome report.

No pilot metric approves compliance or closes Finding/CAPA.
