# Sprint 12 Institutional Committee Checklist

1. Approve the committee charter and conflict-of-interest policy.
2. Verify member authority, training, term dates and role mapping.
3. Record attendance and signed references.
4. Confirm quorum before opening the session.
5. Record conflict declarations for every attended voting member and agenda item.
6. Resolve or recuse every conflict before decision entry.
7. Record a rationale for each committee decision.
8. Perform independent decision-quality review.
9. Generate the decision metrics snapshot and review SLA breaches.
10. Do not interpret these controls as accreditation scoring or automated approval.
