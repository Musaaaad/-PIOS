# Sprint 12 Institutional Committee Runbook

1. Approve the committee charter and conflict-of-interest policy.
2. Add members, verify authority and training, and activate the committee.
3. Create or bind an Intelligence Review Session.
4. Record attendance and signed references.
5. Declare conflict status for every voting member and agenda item; resolve or recuse conflicts.
6. Evaluate governance readiness before opening the session.
7. Record decisions with rationale using an attended and authorized actor.
8. Perform independent decision-quality review.
9. Generate SLA and decision-metrics snapshots.
10. Preserve evidence references and audit events.

No committee metric substitutes for compliance determination or accreditation evidence.
