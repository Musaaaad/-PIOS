# Sprint 9 - Risk Intelligence and Human-Governed AI

## Scope
- Deterministic risk assessment across 266 measurable elements.
- Contextual executive portfolio across active sites.
- Governed recommendations with mandatory human review.

## Non-delegable decisions
The AI layer cannot approve compliance, approve Not Applicable, sign evidence, close critical CAPA, change KPI definitions, or publish EvidenceReady.

## Methodology
Risk combines explicit signals: ESR, open findings, readiness state, overdue assurance controls, and data-quality issues. It is not a clinical prediction or CBAHI score.
